Evan Collins

CV
I'm a biological engineering PhD candidate at MIT advised by Dan Anderson and Bob Langer. I work at the intersection of biochemical engineering and data science to build better nanomedicines. I'm a Siebel Scholar, MIT HEALS fellow, and MIT Jameel Clinic fellow.

I completed my bachelors and masters in biomedical engineering at Yale. I've previously worked at Click Therapeutics, D. E. Shaw, and Simplex Sciences.

My other research interests include systems neuroscience—where I helped build and actively maintain the Yale Brain Atlas—and cognitive psychology, where I helped identify the implied truth effect.
